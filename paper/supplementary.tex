bands group the eleven assignments as CS1, Codeforces 800--1000, and Codeforces
1200--2000; the last band folds C6's three submissions in rather than reporting
them alone.

\section{Rule Provenance}

The builder authored 91 rules that fired at least once, against the shared
generic library's 5; they fired 789 times against the library's 293. The
distinction that matters is reuse. Exactly one authored rule fired on more than
one assignment, while \texttt{generic\_flip\_compare} alone fired 198 times
across 8 of the 11 assignments, \texttt{generic\_shift\_range} 43 times across
6, and \texttt{generic\_shift\_index} 16 times across 5. The authored layer is
assignment-specific by construction, which is why it is priced per assignment
and the generic layer is not.

The most-fired authored rules are single-assignment and legible as
misconceptions rather than as patches: \texttt{slice\_case\_check\_to\_equality}
(115 firings), \texttt{count\_compare\_relax} (112),
\texttt{append\_missing\_return\_len\_after\_loop} (108),
\texttt{capitalize\_title\_to\_swapcase} (45), and
\texttt{undefined\_domain\_var\_rename} (28). On C4 a single rule,
\texttt{carry\_fix\_two\_value\_print}, repairs 27 of the assignment's 38
submissions.

The rules self-learning absorbed read the same way:
\texttt{uniqueness\_count\_gt1\_to\_neq1},
\texttt{counter\_var\_confusion},
\texttt{index\_vs\_count\_and\_uncalled\_bool} and
\texttt{missing\_else\_false} on R2 with the frontier builder;
\texttt{count\_equality\_for\_uniqueness},
\texttt{count\_uniqueness\_instead\_of\_direct\_membership} and
\texttt{missing\_call\_and\_predicate\_fix} on R2 with the cheap one; and
\texttt{binarysearch\_zero\_exit} on C3.

\section{Released Artifacts}

The repository contains the harness, the dataset-import pipeline, every prompt,
the synthesized repairers and their build logs, the raw model responses, and the
per-submission event logs each arm wrote. Four commands regenerate every number
in both documents from those logs: \texttt{run.py stats} (Wilson intervals and
exact McNemar), \texttt{run.py tables}, \texttt{run.py numbers}, and
\texttt{run.py figures}. The 29 measured threshold settings are archived as
\texttt{results/v2/threshold\_sweep.json}.
