\section*{Reproducibility Checklist}
\noindent\textbf{General paper structure.} Conceptual outline of the approach: yes (Figure~2 and the Approach section). Claims vs.\ speculation delineated: yes; the break-even expression in RQ1 is derivation over measured quantities and is marked as such. Pedagogical references for background: yes (Related Work).

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Theoretical contributions.} None. The break-even expression in RQ1 is elementary algebra over measured quantities, with every symbol defined in the text.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Datasets.} Two publicly available corpora: the Refactory dataset \citep{refactory2019} and Codeforces problems and submissions from the open-r1 release \citep{penedo2025codeforces}. We state the problem-selection rule we applied, so the evaluation sets are reconstructible from the public corpora, and the per-submission event logs for every arm are released. No new human-subject data are collected; the corpora carry submission identifiers from their sources, which we did not re-identify.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Computational experiments.} Source code, every prompt, the 22 synthesized repairers with their build costs, and the per-submission event logs are released; everything except the model calls runs on CPU. Model versions, pricing snapshot, the baseline's sampling budget ($K{=}10$ candidates per submission), execution timeouts, and the no-split whole-pool protocol are stated in Experimental Setup. Local search and evaluation are deterministic; the escalation threshold is a constant rather than a fitted quantity, so no configuration is selected on the evaluation set, and the 29 alternatives we measured are reported in the supplement rather than used to choose one. Every table, figure and in-text number is regenerated from the released logs by \texttt{run.py tables}, \texttt{figures}, \texttt{numbers} and \texttt{stats}; \texttt{run.py numbers} additionally reports whether each recomputed value still occurs verbatim in the paper.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{What is not reproducible.} Both builders are closed commodity models reached through provider APIs, so re-running a build is not guaranteed to synthesize the same rules. What the artifact fixes is the output of the builds we ran: the tools are ordinary Python and repair offline without a model. Repair rates and per-pair exact McNemar tests are computed by \texttt{run.py stats} and written to \texttt{results/stats.csv}; the paper reports the effect sizes rather than the test statistics.
